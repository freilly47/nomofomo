# Implementation outcomes

- [ ] **Deliver a NoMo FOMO contact page that carries over the supplied homepage’s branding, typography, colors, spacing, and visual language.** Include navigation between the supplied homepage and the contact page.
- [ ] **Deliver an accessible contact form with name, email, subject, and message fields.** Provide clear labels, required-field cues, and an understandable form structure.
- [ ] **Deliver client-side validation with clear inline error, submission, and success states.** Required fields and invalid email addresses must be identified beside the relevant field; valid submission must show feedback without transmitting personal information.
- [ ] **Deliver a responsive presentation for mobile, tablet, and desktop.** The header, contact context, form fields, feedback, and footer must remain readable and usable at each viewport.
