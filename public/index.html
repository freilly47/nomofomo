The idea

FOMO gets expensive fast.

Students want to see friends and make memories, but limited budgets can make every group-chat suggestion feel like pressure to spend.

The problem

A social life should not require a big budget.

There are plenty of things to do around Dublin, but searching takes time, prices can add up, and agreeing on a plan with friends is awkward. The result is often missing out—or overspending.

Our solution

A small plan, built around real constraints.

NoMo FOMO pairs budget, interests, time and transport with affordable social activities, then makes a clear invitation people can drop straight into the group chat.

What we are building

Five features. One useful loop.

Each feature exists to get a student from “I have no idea” to “I’ll send that now.”

Small enough for a working hackathon demo
01

Quickfire Matching

A short set of choices: what students want to do, social energy, budget, time and transport.

No accounts. No long forms.

02

Curated Recommendations

Three relevant Dublin/DCU-focused ideas, from gigs and run clubs to book clubs, food, fitness and free outdoor plans.

Cost and availability stay clearly estimated.

03

Make a Plan

A selected activity becomes a compact itinerary with meeting point, timing, total-spend estimate, what to bring and booking guidance.

Built for the group chat, not a spreadsheet.

04

FOMO Vault

A simple save-for-later space for ideas worth revisiting: free plans, move and train, meet people, this week, and gigs to watch.

No idea gets lost in the chat.

05

Ticket Ready

For ticketed plans, students see official-source guidance, full-night cost reminders and prompts to follow venues and promoters.

Never claims live tickets or presale access.

The product journey

From free evening to actual plan.

The prototype is deliberately focused: one quick journey that a judge can understand in seconds.

  1. 01 / INPUT

  2. 02 / MATCH

    See three useful ideas

    Each explains the fit, including cost, time, social format and what to bring.

  3. 03 / SHARE

    Make the move

    Choose an activity, generate a simple plan, then copy the friendly invitation.

Why it matters

Social life, not social pressure.

The project makes socialising feel achievable without spending beyond a student budget. It pushes local experiences, shared costs and lower-impact ways of getting around.
